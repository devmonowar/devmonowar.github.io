Alo Blog

A quiet theme for people who write.

A lightweight personal blog theme: single-column reading list, right sidebar on blog and single posts, threaded comments, translation-ready and RTL-friendly. Accessibility-first, reader-first typography, and zero external requests.

What it does not do

  • No page builder, no slider, no upsells
  • No tracking, no bundled scripts, no external requests
  • Four theme options, not a second dashboard
